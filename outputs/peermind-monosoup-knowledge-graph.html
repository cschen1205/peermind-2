How to read this

Every edge has provenance

Select a node or edge. Paper claims are reported as claims; reviewer concerns are judgments; author replies are responses. None is silently converted into ground truth.

Source boundary. The paper graph uses the stable arXiv v1 manuscript. The review layer uses the human OpenReview discussion. OpenReview’s page was browser-gated during extraction, so note identities and text were recovered from the indexed public forum. The arXiv edition is not asserted to be identical to every review-time revision.
